- **Trade account:** your name, email address, phone number, company name and country, and your password (stored only in encrypted form).

## How we use your information
- To answer your enquiries and send quotations.
- To create and manage your trade account and show prices you are entitled to see.
- To process and deliver orders.
- To send account emails, such as email verification and approval notices.
- To protect the website against spam, fraud and misuse.
- To send our newsletter, only if you subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Sharing your information
We share information only when needed to provide our service: for example with shipping companies delivering your order, our email and hosting providers, and, if you use the website chat, the AI service that generates its replies. We may also share information when the law requires it.

## Cookies and similar technologies
We use essential cookies to keep you signed in, protect forms against misuse and remember your quote list. Your colour theme preference is stored in your browser. We do not use advertising cookies.

## Product images

## How long we keep information
We keep account, quotation and order records for as long as your account is active and as long as needed for our business, accounting and legal obligations. Contact messages are kept while they are needed to answer you.
