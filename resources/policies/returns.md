## Reporting a problem
- Inspect your order as soon as it arrives.
- Report damage, shortages or defects within **7 days of delivery** by emailing {email}.
- Include your order or quotation reference, a description of the problem and photos of the affected items and packaging.
- Please keep the goods and packaging until your claim is resolved.

## What we cover
- Items damaged in transit.
- Manufacturing defects, such as faulty stitching, hardware or finishing.
- Items that do not match your confirmed order or approved sample.
- Missing items.

## What we do not cover
- Natural variations in leather grain, shade, texture and markings, which are characteristics of genuine leather.
- Wear and tear, misuse or damage after delivery.
- Custom, branded and private-label items made to your approved specification or sample, except for manufacturing defects.
- Change-of-mind returns on wholesale orders.

## How claims are resolved
After reviewing your claim, we will replace or repair the affected items, credit them against your next order, or refund them. If goods need to be returned, we will tell you how and cover the return shipping for valid claims. Returns sent without our agreement cannot be accepted.

## Cancellations
Orders can be changed or cancelled before production starts. Once production of a custom or private-label order has started, it cannot be cancelled.
